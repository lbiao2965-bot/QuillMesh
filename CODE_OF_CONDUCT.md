# Community conduct

QuillMesh contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces constructive, respectful, and focused on the work.

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome users of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith while still addressing safety concerns directly.
- Do not publish private documents, personal information, credentials, or harassment.
- Respect a maintainer's request to pause or move an unproductive discussion.

Unacceptable behavior may be edited, removed, or result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces. To report conduct that should not be discussed publicly, ask a maintainer for a private contact channel without posting sensitive details.
